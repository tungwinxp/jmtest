Arena: Rapid and accurate reconstruction of full-atomic RNA structures from
coarse-grained models

Zion R. Perry, Anna Marie Pyle, Chengxin Zhang

Source code and benchmark data published at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18963141 under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

`../nucleic.rs` and `tables.rs` are adapted from that source: the algorithm is
rewritten in Rust and its numeric tables are copied. `README.md` lists the
changes. `jmfs/fixtures/rna/` holds one structure of the benchmark data and
Arena's output for it. The material is provided by its authors without
warranties, as that licence states.
