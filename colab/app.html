What is reduced chemistry?

Reduced allows substitutions within JMFS’s predefined residue groups at the chemistry-gated positions. Protein groups are A/S/T, R/K, N/D, Q/E, C/U, I/V, L/M and F/Y; G, H, P and W stay separate. For example, serine can match alanine or threonine, and aspartate can match asparagine. Modified residues follow the engine’s normalization.

Exact requires the same normalized residue identity. None disables chemistry filtering. A blank chemistry-gated field checks every motif residue. Segment continuity, geometry and the RMSD cutoff still apply. Reduced groups do not establish equivalent catalytic activity.

For nucleic acids, reduced groups are A/G/I and C/U/T. Protein residues and nucleotide bases never substitute for each other.
